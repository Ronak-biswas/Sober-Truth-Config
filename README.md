## 🌱 Why This Exists

Every year, thousands of users stumble into forums promising "insane FPS gains" or "latency annihilation" after editing a dozen registry keys they can't explain. A week later, their system stutters, their fans scream, and their trust in tuning is gone.

**SoberOptimizations Refined** exists to answer a simple, elegant question:

> *What if we only changed the things that actually matter — and we documented exactly why?*

Instead of chasing that mythical "1% extra", this repository focuses on:

- **Reversible changes** — every tweak has an undo path.
- **Measured impact** — benchmarks before, benchmarks after.
- **Honest constraints** — we tell you what *doesn't* work and never will.
- **Real-world gains** — improvements you can feel on a mid-range machine, not just a benchmarking rig.

---

## 🧭 Project Philosophy

1. **No Placebo Theater.** If a setting cannot be linked to a measurable, repeatable change, it doesn't ship.
2. **Reversibility Above All.** Any script that touches system state must produce a rollback artifact.
3. **Transparency.** Every recommendation is annotated with *what it does*, *who it helps*, and *who it might harm*.
4. **No Cult of Personality.** We don't care who "invented" a tweak; we care whether it survives scrutiny.
5. **Frugal by Default.** The best optimization is often the bottleneck you didn't create in the first place.
6. **Sustainable Performance.** A cooler CPU today beats a hotter, faster, throttling CPU tomorrow.
7. **Community-Driven Rigor.** Anyone can submit a change — as long as they bring proof.

## 🏛️ Core Pillars

### 1. Evidence Before Ideology
Every tweak in this repository earned its place by surviving a review process. Contributors submit a change along with a hypothesis, a method, and results. If the results are ambiguous, the tweak stays in the "experimental" folder — clearly labeled, never in the default profile.

### 2. Narrative Documentation
We don't just list options; we tell their story. Each entry answers:
- What problem does this solve?
- What is the underlying mechanism?
- Who has reported the most reliable gains?
- What are the known side effects?

### 3. Small Capsules, Big Picture
Instead of monolithic "performance packs", we ship small, composable capsules. You can adopt a thermal capsule, a latency capsule, and a battery capsule independently — or stack them if your hardware can take it.

### 4. Rollback as a First-Class Citizen
The moment a profile is applied, a rollback bundle is created. It's not an afterthought; it's a sibling artifact. Restoring the previous state takes a single command, and the ledger records the reason.

## 🖥️ Responsive UI & Multilingual Support

The companion dashboard is designed for real desks and real hands. Its layout reflows across sizes, from a compact window tucked into the corner of a small laptop to a full-bleed view on a wide monitor. Components shrink, stack, or expand depending on available space, and text remains legible without you reaching for the zoom control.

**Responsive design goals:**
- Fluid grid that reflows between breakpoints without content jumping.
- Contrast-safe typography that respects accessibility preferences.
- Reduced-motion mode for users who find animation distracting.
- Keyboard-first navigation so power users rarely touch the pointing device.

**Multilingual support goals:**
- Language packs stored separately from logic, so translators never need to touch behavior code.
- Right-to-left layout handling baked into the UI shell, not bolted on later.
- A translation status page so the community can see which strings are pending.
- Locale-aware number and date formatting for benchmark outputs.

These two features work together: a translated interface is only useful if it's also legible on the device you actually own — which is why the responsive work and the multilingual work ship in the same release cadence.

## 🕓 24/7 Customer Support Model

We can't promise a literal help desk staffed around the clock — and we won't pretend to. What we *can* promise is a **rotation-based triage model** that approximates round-the-clock coverage:

- **Async-first**: Most questions get answered within one day, regardless of your timezone.
- **Rotation schedule**: Maintainers volunteer for windows; overlapping coverage smooths the handoffs.
- **Self-serve first**: A searchable knowledge module answers the majority of routine questions instantly.
- **Escalation path**: Genuine bugs get a tracked ticket and a status page.
- **Onboarding buddy**: New users are paired with a mentor for their first two weeks.

The goal isn't a theatrical promise of "always-on humans" — it's a resilient system that never leaves a contributor stranded.

## 🚦 Getting Started Without the Goosebumps

1. **Read the Philosophy section** — understand the mindset before you touch anything.
2. **Run the Sanity Linter** — it reports on your current configuration without changing it.
3. **Pick a single capsule** — start small. The thermal capsule is a gentle introduction.
4. **Apply it in a test window** — the dashboard creates a snapshot automatically.
5. **Measure** — run the bundled benchmark harness before and after.
6. **Decide** — keep, adjust, or revert using the rollback bundle.
7. **Log it** — the change ledger records what you did and why.

There's no rush. The whole point of a sober approach is that you can take your time and still arrive at a better place.

## 📈 Performance Metrics & How We Measure

Performance is not a vibe; it's a distribution. Our harness records:

- **Frame-time consistency** rather than raw averages, because stutter hides in the tails.
- **Thermal steady-state** after fifteen minutes, not the first sixty seconds.
- **Idle power draw** as a proxy for whether background churn was actually addressed.
- **Cold-start latency** for common tasks, measured across multiple runs.
- **Responsiveness under memory pressure**, since that's where many systems quietly suffer.

Each capsule ships with a `baseline/after` pair of reports and a short narrative explaining the delta. If the delta is within noise, we say so plainly.

## 🛡️ Safety & Reversibility

- Every change is paired with a rollback artifact created at apply-time.
- The linter refuses to proceed if a capsule would conflict with an existing setting.
- A fail-safe mode halts execution if the environment doesn't match expectations.
- No default capsule disables or weakens malware protection, firewall rules, or update mechanisms.
- The ledger is append-only, so there's always an audit trail.

We treat your machine like a shared workshop: tidy up after yourself, and leave a note for the next person.

## 🤝 Contribution Guidelines

- **Bring evidence.** Anecdotes are welcome as discussion starters, but shipping requires measurement.
- **Document the "why".** A change without rationale is a change waiting to be reverted.
- **Keep capsules small.** Large, opaque bundles are rejected on principle.
- **Translate generously.** Localization contributions are reviewed quickly and credited in the changelog.
- **Respect the tone.** No hype, no shaming, no "trust me bro".
- **Sign your work.** A name in the ledger helps the community route questions.

Reviewers aim to respond to new proposals within a week. If a proposal needs more data, we'll tell you exactly which numbers would move it forward.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is this just the original guide with a new coat of paint?**
No. The original guide paved the way; this project builds a full workshop on top of it.

**Do I need to be technical?**
Not particularly. The dashboard and documentation are written for curious users, not just engineers.

**Will this make my system faster?**
If a bottleneck exists, we help you find it. If one doesn't, we'll tell you honestly.

**Can I undo everything?**
Yes. Rollback bundles exist for precisely that reason.

**Why the emphasis on multilingual support?**
Because performance advice shouldn't be gated behind a single language.

**How is support handled?**
Through a rotation-based triage model with async-first responses and a self-serve knowledge module.
